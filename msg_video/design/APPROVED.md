# Approved visual style: Kombu Paper

Approved by the client on 2026-10-27, from the 9:16 style frames in `design/frames/kombu-paper/` (A, B, C), "exactly as shown".

Binding for all animation:
- Style frames A/B/C are the reference. The palette, paper and riso texture, ink colour, characters (shadow-puppet talking heads, Goldie the broth droplet, gut cells with bibs) and caption treatment are as rendered there.
- Drawing helpers live in `design/frames/kombu-paper/frames.js` (`window.KP`); the scene kit is built from them.
- Captions: torn white paper strip, Nunito Black, spoken words in ink, upcoming words in lavender, the current word on a raised chip coloured by act (pink in the cold open, gold on glutamate, red in the 1960s section).
- Client rules from BRIEF.md still apply: vertical 1080x1920, at most one 1–3 word label besides captions, only vivid saturated colours.
- Format: a single Instagram Reel under 3 minutes (PARTS in script.py), narration in a Kurzgesagt-like writing voice.

Directions not chosen: Afterglow (frames unfinished, stopped) and Lunchbox Lab (kept in design/frames for reference).
