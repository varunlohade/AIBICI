# MSG — Production Plan (one Reel, one animator, Kombu Paper, story-first cut)

Companion to `design/SHOTLIST.md` and `design/shotlist.json`. Both are generated by `python3 design/gen_shotlist.py`.
The Reel is 129.0 s, with 8 scenes, 40 shots and 17 hero moments. It is one vertical 1080x1920 Reel that loops.

**Scene order** (client-approved after the second viewer test, "you buried your best story"):
s01 → **s06** → s03 → s04 → s05 → s07 → s08 → s09. The s02 title card is cut (a viewer said a title at 0:51 "felt
like the video was starting over"); `scenes/s02_title.js` stays in the repo, unused.

## 0. Open items (producer)

1. **The real clips are in.** They are two clips of Dr. Russell Blaylock, credited "Neurosurgeon, lecture on MSG,
   2012", and now play in this order:
   - line 1: `blaylock_hungry`
   - line 2: `blaylock_brain`

   Both are drawn with `CLIPS.draw` from `lib/clips.js`. The client must still clear rights, exact attribution and
   context.
2. **Clip size needs a still sign-off.** The client asked for the clip to be big, so s01.01–02 push the camera in until
   the hero screen fills most of TOP+MID. Get sign-off on a still of s01.01 at the new framing. Any treatment of the
   clip must not change what the speaker says or how he comes across.
3. **Captions** come from `KP.captionLayer` in `player.html`. The strip must stay inside y 1300–1480. At the new
   framing, the big screen and the name lower third must both clear the band.
4. **Loop.** s09's last frame must equal s01's frame 0: the big hero screen showing `blaylock_hungry` frame 0, with the
   lower third.
5. **No blank transitions.** The last test caught two paper slides that flashed an empty colour. Every slide now
   carries the next shot's art, and the generator rejects any slide transition that doesn't say what it carries.
   The scene joins are:
   - s01→s06: match cut on the envelope and desk.
   - s06→s03: match on the gold drop, which lands in Ikeda's soup bowl.
   - Act changes: Gold Ripple wipe.
   - Everything else: paper slides that carry content.

**Order and reasons:**

- Do s01 first (the hook), then s06, which is now second in the Reel and carries the story.
- Then re-time s05.
- Then s03, s04, s07 and s08.
- Do s09 last, because its last frame has to match s01's frame 0.

**Working rules:**

- **Build each prop once in the kit.** Use `lib/kp.js` for props and `lib/clips.js` for footage.
- **Animate every listed beat.** SFX, label times and beats keep any stretch without a visible action at 2 s or
  less; `max_still` in the JSON is at most 2.0 for every shot.
- **Hero stills before animating.** Send the producer stills of:
  - s01.01 (big clip framing)
  - s01.05 → s06.01 (match cut)
  - s06.05
  - s06.08 (bell contrast)
  - s06.09 (stamp on the carton)
  - s06.10 → s03.01 (drop into the bowl)
  - s05.02
  - s09.05 (loop)

**Per-scene loop:**

1. Re-time or block the scene against `shotlist.json`, with the safe-zone overlay on.
2. Run `node render.js --contact <id> 12`, then `--stills <id> <hero times>`.
3. Run `--video <id> --jobs 1` and then the QA checklist below.
4. At every scene join, render the last frame of one scene next to the first frame of the next.

## 2. QA checklist — run on every finished scene (and once on the assembled Reel)

**Viewer-test rules**
- [ ] **Hook:** frame 0 is fully lit, with the clip big on screen and the name lower third, and it grips within 2 s.
- [ ] **No still frames:** nothing holds without a visible action for more than about 2 s; every listed beat happens.
- [ ] **No blank transitions:** step through every transition frame by frame. No frame is an empty solid colour or
      a bare sheet; every slide carries the next shot's art.
- [ ] **Story order:** s06 plays second, and its last line, "Is MSG actually bad for you?", hands straight into
      s03's Ikeda opening (gold drop into the bowl) with no dead air and no title card.
- [ ] **"The fear stuck to one cuisine"** (s06.09) is a strong, full frame: carton, stamped headline, newspapers and
      bells. It is never a near-empty frame.
- [ ] **No edge crops:** nothing important within 40 px of the key-box edges (x 40–940, y 220–1300). This matters
      most for the big clip framing.
- [ ] **Seaweed** (s03, s06.01) is recognisable in a 50% still.
- [ ] **Mice** are on a lab tray, never a plate.
- [ ] **Bell (s06.08):** big swings on the carton; the parmesan bell is completely still.

**Frame and text**
- [ ] Nothing that must be read or seen sits in y 0–220, y 1480–1920 or x 940–1080. The caption band is free.
- [ ] At most one 1–3 word label at a time; the only extra text is the credit line under "Russell Blaylock".
      Text is 40 px or larger.

**Real clips** (s01.01–02)
- [ ] Each clip plays unaltered and big inside the hero screen, with clip time following `CLIPS.lineStart`, and stays
      in sync.
- [ ] Name and credit are from `CLIPS.info` and stay on screen while the face is readable.
- [ ] No cut-out covers the speaker.
- [ ] Captions match the clip audio.

**Style (Kombu Paper)**
- [ ] Matches style frames A/B/C: cut paper, hard offset shadows, riso misregistration, grain 10–12%, boil on twos.
- [ ] No outlines, glows or gradients; vivid colours only; gold only on glutamate (and the s06→s03 gold drop).
- [ ] Shared props come from `lib/kp.js`.

**Timing and sound**
- [ ] Shot boundaries are within ±2 frames of the shot list.
- [ ] Hero beats land on their word:
  - stamp on "Chinese" (s06.05)
  - stamp on "stuck" (s06.09)
  - "?" on "right?" (s06.10)
  - 95% on "ninety-five"
  - GRAS on "generally"
  - NO going out on "sign..."
  - "MSG" end card on "shaker"
- [ ] `sfx()` uses only BRIEF.md names, with `t` within 0..D and gains 0..1.

**Technical**
- [ ] Deterministic, with byte-identical stills. Only `scenes/<id>.js` and the shared kit are edited.
- [ ] The Reel is under 3:00 (currently 129.0 s). Played on loop, the s09→s01 seam is invisible.

**Facts and ethics**
- [ ] Every on-screen claim or number is on the shot list's "Allowed" list; never "5%".
- [ ] s06 is paper and objects only:
  - no restaurant, staff or diners
  - no faux-Asian fonts or signifiers, and a plain white carton
  - the headline quoted and framed as the injustice, including where it is stamped on the carton in s06.09
  - no needle touching the mice
- [ ] A second reviewer signs off s06.
