# MSG — Production Plan (one Reel, one animator, Kombu Paper, viewer-test cut)

Companion to `design/SHOTLIST.md` and `design/shotlist.json`. Both are generated by `python3 design/gen_shotlist.py`.
The Reel is 138.4 s, with 9 scenes, 43 shots and 17 hero moments. It is one vertical 1080x1920 Reel that loops.

## 0. Open items (producer)

1. **The real interview clips are pending from the client.** `lib/clips.js` is ready:
   - `CLIPS.draw` returns false until `clips/prepared/` exists, so s01 shows a placeholder rectangle until then.
   - Once the clips arrive, s01 lines 1–3 must be re-cued from the clip audio, then `gen_shotlist.py` re-run.
   - The client must clear rights, exact attribution (name and credit line) and context for each clip.
2. **Real footage inside a paper TV is a new design choice.** BRIEF.md says new design choices go to the client.
   Get sign-off on a still of s01.01 with a real clip before animating further. Any treatment of the clip (crop,
   riso tint) must not change what the person says or how they come across.
3. **Captions** are drawn by `KP.captionLayer` in `player.html`. Check once that the strip sits inside y 1300–1480 and
   that the clip lines' captions match the real clip audio.
4. **Edges and loop.** s01 opens lit on frame 0. s09's last frame matches s01's frame 0 layout, with the hero TV
   empty and a flicker into clip 1. There are no dips to black anywhere.

**s01 changes in detail:**

  shaker, zig-zags) sit beside the screen, never on the person.
- **Lower third:** the person's name plus a credit line (outlet, date), drawn from `CLIPS.info`. No DRAMATIZATION.
- **s01.04–05:** same content. They are now timed to "everyone has been scared of MSG", and a lemon envelope arrives
  on "it all started".
- **New s01.06 flash-forward** (1.7 s, from 17.31 to 18.99 s):
  - Smash cut to the s06.05 headline stamp, landing on "1968".
  - Use a nested draw of s06 at its stamp time, so it stays in sync with s06.
- **Old shots 6 and 7 merged into s01.07:**
  - It starts with a record scratch on the cut back.
  - The shaker and its "?" follow ("is it actually bad for you?").
  - The old "And if it isn't... why" record-scratch beat is gone.

**Order and reasons:**

- Rework s01 first, because it is the hook and the flash-forward depends on s06.05.
- Then rework s05 and s06, and re-time s02.
- Then rework s03 and build s04, s07 and s08.
- Build s09 last, because its final frame must match s01's frame 0.

**Working rules:**

- **Build each prop once in the kit.** Use `lib/kp.js` for props and `lib/clips.js` for footage.
- **Secondary beats.** Every shot lists SFX, label times and secondary-action beats. Animate each beat: these are
  what keep any stretch without a visible action at 2 s or less.
- **Hero stills before animating.** Send the producer stills of:
  - s01.01 with a real clip, or the placeholder
  - s01.06 (flash-forward)
  - s05.02
  - s06.05
  - s06.08 (bell contrast)
  - s09.05 (loop)

**Per-scene loop:**

1. Re-time or block the scene against `shotlist.json`, with the safe-zone overlay on.
2. Run `node render.js --contact <id> 12`, then `--stills <id> <hero times>`.
3. Run `--video <id> --jobs 1`, then the QA checklist below.
4. For s09, put its last frame next to s01's frame 0.

## 2. QA checklist — run on every finished scene (and once on the assembled Reel)

**Viewer-test rules**
- [ ] **Hook:** frame 0 is fully lit, and the hero TV, clip 1 (or placeholder) and name lower third read by 0.3 s.
      The Reel is gripping within 2 s.
- [ ] **No still frames:** nothing holds without a visible action for more than about 2 s. Scrub the video and check
      every beat in the shot list happens. `max_still` in the JSON is at most 2.0 for every shot.
- [ ] **No edge crops:** nothing important within 40 px of the key-box edges (x 40–940, y 220–1300). Check the contact
      sheet with the overlay on.
- [ ] **Seaweed** (s03, s06.01) is recognisable as seaweed in a 50% still with no caption.
- [ ] **Mice** are on a lab tray with a rim and label tab, never a plate (s06.06–07).
- [ ] **Bell (s06.08):** big swings and shake on the carton; the parmesan bell is completely still (no boil).

**Frame and text**
- [ ] Nothing that must be read or seen sits in y 0–220, y 1480–1920 or x 940–1080. The caption band is free.
- [ ] At most one 1–3 word label at a time, with in/out times matching the shot list. The only extra text is the
      credit line under a real name.
- [ ] Text is 40 px or larger; no subtitle duplicates the caption.

**Real clips** (s01)
- [ ] The clip plays unaltered inside the hero screen. Clip time follows `CLIPS.lineStart`, and the audio and picture
      stay in sync.
- [ ] Name and credit come from `CLIPS.info` and are on screen whenever a face is readable.
- [ ] No cut-out overlaps the person.
- [ ] Captions match the clip audio.

**Style (Kombu Paper)**
- [ ] Matches style frames A/B/C: cut paper, hard offset shadows, riso misregistration on hero shapes, grain 10–12%,
      boil on twos.
- [ ] No outlines, glows or gradients. Vivid colours only.
- [ ] Gold appears only on glutamate.
- [ ] Shared props (Goldie, gut cells with bibs, the Fifth Ripple, torn-paper slips, stamp, kombu) come from `lib/kp.js`.
- [ ] Transitions: paper slide, Gold Ripple on act changes, channel flicker on the TV wall; the flash-forward is a
      smash cut in and out.

**Timing and sound**
- [ ] Shot boundaries are within ±2 frames of the shot list.
- [ ] Hero beats land on their word:
  - stamp on "1968" in s01.06
  - stamp on "Chinese" in s06.05
  - 95% on "ninety-five"
  - GRAS on "generally"
  - NO going out on "sign..."
  - "MSG" end card on "shaker"
- [ ] `sfx()` uses only BRIEF.md names, with `t` within 0..D and gains 0..1. Only hero impacts go above 0.7.

**Technical**
- [ ] Deterministic: no `Math.random`, `Date` or state between frames. The same still renders byte-identical twice.
- [ ] Only `scenes/<id>.js` and the shared kit are edited.
- [ ] The Reel is under 3:00 (currently 138.4 s). Played on loop, the seam from s09's last frame to s01's frame 0 is
      clean.

**Facts and ethics**
- [ ] Every on-screen claim or number is on the shot list's "Allowed" list.
- [ ] Nothing cut by this rewrite appears:
  - tongue/protein, amino acid, mushrooms, sugar, tomato sauce in s06
  - "savory", 1969, the journal's name
  - never "5%"
- [ ] s06 is paper and objects only:
  - no restaurant, staff or diners
  - no faux-Asian fonts or signifiers, and a plain white carton
  - the headline quoted and framed as criticised, also in the s01 flash-forward
  - no needle touching the mice
- [ ] Ikeda is drawn respectfully.
- [ ] A second reviewer signs off s06 and the s01 flash-forward.
