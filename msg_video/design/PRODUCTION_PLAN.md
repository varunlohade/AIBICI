# MSG — Production Plan (Instagram Reel series, 9:16, 3 parts)

Companion to `design/SHOTLIST.md` / `design/shotlist.json` (89 shots, 21 hero moments), both generated by
`python3 design/gen_shotlist.py`. Style-agnostic: works for whichever direction the client approves.

## 0. Blockers for the producer (before animation starts)

1. **Canvas is still 16:9.** `lib/kz.js` (`W = 1920, H = 1080`) and `player.html` (`<canvas width="1920" height="1080">`)
   must become 1080x1920, and `render.js` / `build.sh` must output 1080x1920 and concatenate **per part**
   (`script.PARTS`) rather than one film. This belongs to the kit owner; animators must not patch it themselves.
2. **Captions layer.** Decide how burned-in captions are made. Recommendation: build them **once** as a shared layer
   driven by `cues.json` `lines[].words[]`, drawn last (after the vignette) in the 1300-1480 band. It could be a kit
   helper each scene calls, or a compositing pass at render time. Spec: max 2 lines of about 22 characters, active word
   highlighted on its `start`, line changes on phrase boundaries, no captions during s02 (the title text is the caption).
   Animators only keep the band clear.
3. **Fade rule change.** Part openers (s01, s05, s06) start fully lit on frame 0, and part closers (s04, s08, s09) hold
   their end card to the last frame. This replaces BRIEF.md's fade-every-scene rule for those six edges; internal joins
   keep a 0.3 s dip.
4. **Shared props file.** Approve one shared props file (proposal: `scenes/_props.js`, namespace `window.KZP`; it loads
   before `s0…` because `render.js` loads `scenes/*.js` alphabetically). Each recurring prop is built once by its
   owner below and called by everyone else.

## 2. Animator split — one animator per part

| Animator | Part | Scenes | Shots | Owns (builds the canonical shared prop) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Part 1 | s01, s02 (restyle + recompose), s03, s04 | 34 | TVWALL, DRAMA_TAG, SIGN, GLU usage, SHAKER, NA, TOMATO, TASTE_ICONS, IKEDA, CHAPTER |
| **B** | Part 2 | s05, s07, s08 | 27 | BODY, GUTCELL, BADGE95, BBB, SALT, plus the **series kit**: PART_TAG, END_CARD, the safe-zone/caption-band guide overlay |
| **C** | Part 3 | s06, s09 | 28 | HEADLINE, LETTER; owns the sensitivity pass for s06 |

Why this split:

- Part 1 has the most shots but s02 already exists and s03/s04 are simple.
- Part 3 is the most sensitive, so C has fewer, slower shots.
- B is the lightest on unique story props, so B also builds the series kit.
- If Part 1 slips, a helper takes **s03 + s04** under A's direction. Those scenes share no prop with s01/s02 except
  GLU and SHAKER.

**Cross-part dependencies.** These are the continuity risks. Every one goes through a shared prop, never a redraw.

## 4. QA checklist — run on every finished scene (and once per assembled part)

Commands (once the kit is vertical): `node render.js --contact <id> 12`, `--stills <id> <hero times>`,
`--video <id> --jobs 1`, `--sfx`. Use the safe-zone guide overlay (series kit) for all layout checks; remove it for
final renders.

**Vertical layout and safe zones**
- [ ] Nothing that must be read or seen is in y 0-220, y 1480-1920 or x 940-1080. Only background/ambient bleeds there.
- [ ] The caption band (y 1300-1480) holds no key content or keyword text, and its background stays calm enough for
      captions to be legible.
- [ ] Main subject sits in MID, centred on x≈490; TOP/MID/LOW contents match the shot list; split screens are top/bottom.
- [ ] All on-screen words are at least 40 px; keywords are short (no second subtitle track).

**Hooks, tags and endings** (part openers/closers)
- [ ] Opener: frame 0 is fully lit (not black); PART n/3 tag visible by 0.3 s; the hook's subject and keyword read by
      2.0 s; the tag shrinks to the small pill after about 3 s.
- [ ] Closer (parts 1-2): end card "PART 2 →" / "PART 3 →" lands on the spoken word "part" and holds to the last frame,
      with no fade. Part 3 ends on the held series end card.
- [ ] The part makes sense watched alone; callbacks read without the other parts.

**Technical**
- [ ] Scene id matches `cues.json`; only your own `scenes/<id>.js` (and props you own) edited; no edits to `lib/`,
      `render.js`, `player.html`, `script.py`.
- [ ] Helpers in an IIFE or namespaced; no new top-level globals.
- [ ] Deterministic: no `Math.random`, `Date` or `performance.now`, and no state carried between frames. The same
      still rendered twice is byte-identical (`md5sum`), and stills rendered out of order look right.
- [ ] Internal joins dip 0.3 s; vignette or style finish applied before the caption layer.
- [ ] Never a fully static frame.

**Timing and sound**
- [ ] Shot boundaries within ±2 frames of `shotlist.json`. Those times come from real word timings, so check them
      against the waveform, not by eye.
- [ ] Hero beats land on their word: 95% on "ninety-five", headline stamp on "Chinese", GRAS on "generally", NO burning
      out on "sign", end cards on "part".
- [ ] `sfx()` uses only BRIEF.md names, all `t` within 0..D, gains 0..1. About one SFX every 1-3 s; 0.2-0.6 under
      narration; only hero impacts above 0.7.

**Content and facts**
- [ ] On-screen text matches the shot list exactly. Every number or claim is on the guardrail "Allowed" list:
      no extra numbers, no axis values, no "5%", no "1909", no sodium-reduction %, no health claims.
- [ ] Charts: milk bars about 10:1 (s04.07); MSG vs placebo bars nearly equal and unlabelled (s07.05); Na ions exactly
      3:1 (s08.02).
- [ ] No real logos, mastheads, seals, networks, platforms, brands or recognisable people.

**Ethics and sensitivity**
- [ ] DRAMATIZATION lower-third and TOP pill are on every frame with a talking head (s01.01-06, s09.01, s09.04-06).
- [ ] s06 and every restaurant/takeout image: no people in s06, no caricatures, no "oriental" fonts or signifiers,
      plain white carton, prejudice framed as criticised, headline quoted. A second reviewer signs off s06.
- [ ] Mice scene has no gore; Ikeda/Tokyo is respectful.

**Continuity**
- [ ] Recurring props come from the shared implementation: GLU, SHAKER, SALT, TVWALL, DRAMA_TAG, SIGN, GUTCELL,
      BADGE95, NA, TOMATO, HEADLINE, LETTER, PART_TAG, END_CARD.
- [ ] TV-wall slots and on/off order, and the sign's design and angle, match the continuity bible across parts.

**Style (after approval)**
- [ ] Matches the approved direction's palette, shapes, type and texture rules.
