# MSG — Production Plan (one Reel, one animator, Kombu Paper)

Companion to `design/SHOTLIST.md` and `design/shotlist.json`. Both are generated by `python3 design/gen_shotlist.py`.
The Reel is 179.3 s, with 9 scenes, 50 shots and 17 hero moments. It is one vertical 1080x1920 Reel that loops.

## 0. Before animation starts (producer)

1. **Turn the KP kit into the scene kit.** `window.KP` lives in `design/frames/kombu-paper/frames.js`, and `render.js`
   only loads `lib/kz.js` and `scenes/*.js`. The kit owner needs to make KP available to scenes, either by moving it
   into the kit or adding a scenes-level kit file. The animator must not copy it into every scene.
2. **Caption band mismatch.** The client rule puts captions at y 1300–1480. The style frames' strip sits at about
   1320–1530, which crosses into the Instagram UI zone. `KP.caption` has to be repositioned and sized so two lines of
   Nunito Black fit inside 1300–1480; 64 px may need to drop to about 58 px. Captions are drawn once, from
   `cues.json` `words`, as the last layer. Chip colour follows the act: pink in s01, gold on "glutamate", tomato red
   in s06. Open decision 2 in `NOTES.md` (always-gold chip) is still the client's call.
3. **Loop and edges.** There are no dips to black. s01 opens lit on frame 0, and s09's last frame matches s01's frame
   0 layout. BRIEF.md no longer asks for per-scene fades, so this needs no exception.
4. **`build.sh`** still builds a "full" cut next to each PART. With a single PART called "reel", the full cut is the
   same video as the Reel and can be dropped.
5. **`scenes/s02_title.js`** is the old 16:9 placeholder in the rejected style. Replace it; don't imitate it.

**Why this order:**

- s01, s05 and s06 are the three scenes closest to the approved style frames A, B and C.
- They also build nearly every recurring prop, so later scenes mostly reuse them.
- s06 is the sensitive scene, so it gets reviewed early.
- s09 comes last because its callbacks need to exist and its final frame must match s01's frame 0.

**Working rules for the single animator:**

- **Build each prop once.** Write it the first time a scene needs it, as a shared function in the kit (§0.1), not a
  copy inside the scene. The build-order table above says which scene builds each prop first.
- **Make the hook first and the loop last.**
  - In s01, finish shot s01.01 before anything else.
  - In s09, finish s09.05 last, then check it against s01.01's frame 0 (step 4 of the per-scene loop).
- **Review stills before animating hero shots.** Send stills of each scene's hero shots to the producer first:
  s01.01, s05.04, s06.05, s06.08 and s09.05 are the ones that set the look.

**Per-scene loop:**

1. Block the scene at the shot list's timings with placeholder shapes. Use the safe-zone overlay and the caption
   band mock.
2. Run `node render.js --contact <id> 12`.
3. Swap in the Kombu Paper art and run `--stills <id> <hero times>`.
4. Run `--video <id> --jobs 1` and then the QA checklist below.
5. For the last scene, also render s09's last frame next to s01's frame 0.

## 2. QA checklist — run on every finished scene (and once on the assembled Reel)

**Frame and safe zones**
- [ ] Nothing that must be read or seen sits in y 0–220, y 1480–1920 or x 940–1080. Only decor bleeds there.
- [ ] The caption band (y 1300–1480) is free of key content and its background stays calm under the strip.
- [ ] Main subject is in MID, centred on x≈490, with TOP/MID/LOW contents matching the shot list.

**Text (client rule)**
- [ ] At most one label on screen at a time besides captions, 1–3 words, printed on paper (slip, stamp, sign, lower
      third). Label in/out times match `shotlist.json`.
- [ ] The only extra text is the DRAMATIZATION line in the puppets' lower third.
- [ ] Props carry no lettering unless it is that shot's label.
- [ ] Text is 40 px or larger, and nothing duplicates the caption as a subtitle.

**Style (Kombu Paper, as approved)**
- [ ] Matches style frames A/B/C: cut paper, hard offset shadows (30–50% ink on vivid grounds), riso
      misregistration on hero shapes, grain at 10–12%, boil on twos from `floor(t*15)`.
- [ ] No outlines, glows or gradients.
- [ ] Vivid riso colours only: no grey, beige, cream, muddy brown or desaturated tones. Ink is #1E1033.
- [ ] Gold (#FFB000) appears only on glutamate: Goldie, the flame, the fifth ring, the MSG bar, the gold chip.
- [ ] Recurring characters come from the shared KP implementation: Goldie, gut cells with bibs, shadow puppets, the
      Fifth Ripple, torn-paper slips, the stamp.
- [ ] Transitions: paper slide by default, Gold Ripple on act changes, channel flicker on the TV wall. No black dips.
- [ ] Never a fully static frame.

**Hook and loop** (s01, s09, and the assembled Reel)
- [ ] Frame 0 is fully lit. The TV Doctor puppet, zapping brain and "TV doctor" label all read by 0.3 s.
- [ ] s09's last frame shows the TV wall with the same layout as s01's frame 0. Playing the Reel on loop shows no
      jump, and the channel_switch SFX at the seam is clean.
- [ ] The whole Reel is under 3:00 (currently 179.3 s, with only about 0.7 s of headroom).

**Timing and sound**
- [ ] Shot boundaries are within ±2 frames of the shot list. Those times come from word timings, so check them against
      the waveform.
- [ ] Hero beats land on their word:
  - "TV doctor" at 0.0
  - 95% on "ninety-five"
  - stamp on "Chinese"
  - GRAS on "generally"
  - NO going out on "sign..."
  - "MSG" end card on "shaker"
- [ ] `sfx()` uses only BRIEF.md names, all `t` within 0..D, gains 0..1.
- [ ] About one SFX every 1–3 s; gains 0.3–0.6 under narration; only hero impacts above 0.7.

**Technical**
- [ ] Scene id matches `cues.json`. Only `scenes/<id>.js` and the shared kit file are edited; never `script.py`,
      `render.js` or `player.html`.
- [ ] Deterministic: no `Math.random`, `Date` or `performance.now`, and no state between frames. Rendering the same
      still twice gives byte-identical output, and stills rendered out of order look correct.
- [ ] Helpers live in an IIFE or a namespace, with no new top-level globals.

**Facts and ethics**
- [ ] Every on-screen claim or number is on the shot list's "Allowed" list.
- [ ] Nothing the rewrite cut appears: no Tokyo label, no ~10x milk, no 1969, no journal name, no Italian Restaurant
      Syndrome, no baking powder, no "even water", never "5%".
- [ ] The DRAMATIZATION line is on every frame with a readable puppet. Puppets are generic, with no likeness to real
      people.
- [ ] s06 is paper and objects only:
  - no restaurant, staff or diners
  - no faux-Asian fonts or signifiers, and a plain white carton
  - the headline quoted and framed as the thing being criticised
  - no needle touching the mice
- [ ] Ikeda is drawn respectfully.
- [ ] A second reviewer signs off s06.
