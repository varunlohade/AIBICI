# Lunchbox Lab: designer notes

- **Format:** the frames are composed natively for a **vertical 9:16 Reel (1080x1920)**. The compartment tray is a **vertical stack**: decorative compartments sit under the Reel UI at the top and bottom, the hero compartment fills the safe zone (x 60–940, y 220–1280), and the caption band sits at y 1300–1480.
- **Revision 2 (client rules): minimal text.** Besides the caption, each frame has at most one short label. **A:** "TV DOCTOR" plus the required DRAMATIZATION tag. Channels are wordless: each lid has a vivid colour, a white-dot pattern and an icon badge (heart, note, speech bubble, star, play, colour bars, bolt). **B:** "FUEL!" only. The "you are here" tile is a coiled-gut pictogram, the tongue lid has no label, and the next beats are a blood-drop lid and a brain lid. **C:** the stamped headline plus "1968". The letter body is typewriter squiggles, and three Tomato-underlined squiggles stand in for the symptoms. The suspects are icons with "?" stickers only. "LETTERS" and the headline tag are gone. Space Mono (with its odd "N") is no longer used in any frame. The only typewriter text left is "1968" in Courier Prime Bold.
- **Revision 2: no dull colours.** Tray Grey, Cream and newsprint beige are gone from every surface. **A:** Butter tray, a Tomato TV, a Pool screen with a Butter halo, and every lid and floor in a different saturated palette colour. **B:** a Pool tray on Lilac, with Butter, Pea and Bubblegum compartments, a Lilac gut wall and a Tomato vessel. **C:** "bright paper", meaning a Pool ground, a Butter letter page, Bubblegum, Butter and Pea suspect and column floors, and the Tomato stamp. Captions and stickers use pure white plates. The ink outlines and halftone are unchanged. **Deviation:** the direction's 1960s "colour drains to newsprint" idea is dropped for this act at the client's request. Period feel now comes from the typewriter squiggles, the coarse 14px halftone and the stamp.
- **Caption style (part of the pitch):** a white sticker strip with the hard ink shadow and Atkinson Hyperlegible Bold at about 54–60px. The current word is slapped on a Butter mini-sticker, tilted -3 degrees and scaled up slightly. Spoken words are ink and upcoming words are at 42%. Chunks are 3–4 words. Helper: `LB.caption(ctx, words, index)`.
- **Kit (`LB` in frames.js):** `LB.draw` (6/4/3px ink outline, off-register fill, 0,8 hard ink shadow, halftone crescent), `LB.shade`, `LB.halftone`, `LB.blob`, `LB.hose`, `LB.hand`, `LB.compartment`, `LB.lid` (now with a vivid fill, dot pattern and `icon`), `LB.sticker`, `LB.icon` (wordless badges), `LB.squiggle` (stand-in body text), `LB.col`, and the characters `LB.miniUmi`, `LB.gutCell`, `LB.tvDoc` and `LB.brain`. Everything is deterministic (`LB.rng` → `KZ.rng`). The source file is pure ASCII with `\u` escapes, because the harness serves JS without a charset. Performance: `LB.shade` uses a full-canvas offscreen layer per call, so it should be batched per layer for animation.
- **Guardrails kept:** the TV Doctor is a fictional blank-faced pictogram with invented, wordless channels and the DRAMATIZATION lower-third. Frame C has no cultural imagery and no masthead. The headline appears in quotes as a stamped period artifact. Without the removed "JOURNAL HEADLINE" tag, the critique framing now depends on the narration and the next beats.
- **Open questions for the client:** (1) The DRAMATIZATION tag is 28px as the direction specifies, below the global 40px minimum. Raise it? (2) The lower-third shortens the role name to "TV DOCTOR" to fit the 3-word label rule. Is that acceptable, or must the full "THE DAYTIME TV DOCTOR" stay? (3) Frame C is now full colour. Should the 1968 act still get a distinct palette shift, for example a Lilac or Pool ground, so the history section reads as a different act? (4) The Doctor is Skin Medium. Should the cold-open pictograms rotate through all three tones? (5) With every surface saturated, the risk of reading as kids' TV is higher. Lighter halftone or fewer cheeks would calm Frame B.
