# Lunchbox Lab: designer notes

- **Format change:** after a client request, all three frames are composed natively for a **vertical 9:16 Reel (1080x1920)**, not cropped from 16:9. The compartment tray is now a **vertical stack**: decorative compartments sit under the Reel UI at the top and bottom, the hero compartment fills the safe zone (x 60–940, y 220–1280), and the caption band sits at y 1300–1480. Nothing key goes in the top 220px, the bottom 440px or the right 140px.
- **Caption style (new, part of the pitch):** a Cream sticker strip with the hard ink shadow and Atkinson Hyperlegible Bold at about 54–60px. The current word is slapped on a Butter mini-sticker that is tilted -3 degrees and scaled up slightly. Words already spoken are solid ink and upcoming words are at 42%. Chunks are 3–4 words, in line with the 6-word label rule. Kit helper: `LB.caption(ctx, words, index)`.
- **Kit (`LB` in frames.js, reusable as the scene kit):** `LB.draw` (6/4/3px ink outline, 3px off-register fill, solid 0,8 ink sticker shadow, halftone crescent), `LB.shade` (top-left light, cached 8px/14px dot patterns, also works on unioned shapes), `LB.halftone` (graded dot fields), `LB.blob` (several primitives under one outline), `LB.hose` (rubber-hose limbs), `LB.hand` (3 fingers plus thumb, point/open/grip), `LB.compartment`, `LB.lid`, `LB.sticker`, `LB.col` (6-column grid), plus the characters `LB.miniUmi`, `LB.gutCell`, `LB.tvDoc` and `LB.brain`. Everything is deterministic (`LB.rng` → `KZ.rng`). Performance note: `LB.shade` uses a full-canvas offscreen layer per call. That is fine for stills, but for animation it should be batched per layer, as the director's feasibility notes already say.
- **Frame A deviations:** the spec's "2x3 grid with one compartment spanning 2 cells" became a stack: 2 closed channels, the big Lilac TV compartment, 2 half-open channels, and 4 closed channels below the caption. The half-open lids reveal the *next* speakers by their props only (the Chef's toque and the Influencer's ring light) as foreshadowing. The doctor has a blank face with only a mouth, as the spec asks, and I added a hair cap and ears for readability. The DRAMATIZATION tag is 28px as the direction specifies, which is below the global 40px minimum. **Client decision:** keep it at 28px or raise it to 40px.
- **Frame B additions:** the Umi queue is a tidy serpentine "airport line" that ends at the front cell, which is mid-chomp with squeezed happy eyes, a 1.15x0.85 squash and a Butter spark. The Pool line is the gut barrier. I added a Tomato blood vessel at the bottom with a single tiny Umi in it, which sets up the 95% beat. The tongue inset is in the top row with its lid sliding shut, next to a "SAMPLE 05 / GUT" tile.
- **Frame C guardrails:** only Cream, Liquorice and coarse 14px halftone, with Tomato as the single spot colour. There is no cultural imagery and no real masthead, and the letter text is an original paraphrase, not the real letter. The stamp reads `"CHINESE-RESTAURANT SYNDROME"` with the quotation marks, and I added a small **"JOURNAL HEADLINE, 1968"** tag so the phrase is clearly shown as a quoted period artifact. **Client decision:** keep the tag. The stamp covers the bottom two lines of the letter, but the three underlined symptoms stay legible.
- **Open questions for the client:** (1) the Doctor's skin tone is Skin Medium; should the cold-open pictograms rotate through all three tones? (2) Does the heavy halftone plus thick outline read as "curious adult" enough? Frame B is the most kid-leaning. A calmer option would use lighter dots and fewer cheeks. (3) Should the caption plate change colour per act (for example newsprint Cream in s06, Liquorice in the cold open) or stay Cream everywhere for brand consistency? (4) The decorative compartments under the Reel UI help the tray read at full bleed, but they are busy behind the UI. They could be dimmed to 50%.
